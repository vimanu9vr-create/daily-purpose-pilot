While it’s still today

Do Day One tonight, not tomorrow

Ten minutes now beats an hour on Sunday that never happens. Three things, and the first one is the whole exercise.

  1. Read the opening page. It’s short, and it explains what this actually does — and what it doesn’t.

  2. Do Day 1. One sentence. The real one, not the polished one you’d say out loud to somebody else.

  3. Say it before bed. If you wince slightly, you’ve written the true one. Keep it — that’s the sentence the whole week is built on.
