Why we built it

You didn't fail at manifesting. You ran out of instructions.

Almost everyone who tries this stops within the first week. Not because they stopped wanting it — because Wednesday arrived and there was nothing specific to actually do.

  • You made the vision board. It's still there. Nothing followed it.
  • You downloaded an affirmations app. It sends a nice sentence. You read it and close the app.
  • You bought the journal. You wrote in it four times.
  • You know what you want. You just don't know what today is supposed to look like.

Consistency isn't a personality trait. It's a design problem — and every tool in this category leaves you to solve it alone.

How it works

Four steps, then repeat tomorrow

1. Tell it what you want

In your own words, however messy. "A summer in Europe." "Leave my job by June." "Stop overthinking."

2. It writes for that goal

Your affirmations, short visualisations set in a life where it's already true, and one anchor line to carry through the day.

3. Do the five minutes

A short guided session that ends with one specific action — small enough to actually do before your coffee goes cold.

4. Come back tomorrow

Day two is different from day one. A 21-day journey builds, rather than repeating the same day for three weeks.

Questions

Before you apply

Does it cost anything?

No. Everything is unlocked and free for the two weeks, and we don't ask for a card. If you want to keep it after launch you'll get the founding-tester price — but nothing starts automatically and nothing charges you.

Why do you need my Google Play email?

Google's closed testing works from an email allow-list. We add the exact address you give us, and only that Google account can see the app on the Play Store. If you give us an address that isn't the one you're signed into Play with, the link genuinely won't work for you — this is the single most common way people miss out.

Do I have to open the app every day?

No. Use it on the days it makes sense — that's the only way we learn anything real. What we do ask is that you stay in the testing programme for the full 14 days, which is a separate thing and costs you nothing.

I have an iPhone. Can I test?

Not this round — Google Play closed testing is Android-only. Apply anyway and tick the launch list, and we'll come to you when iOS is ready.

How much time is this really?

Five minutes a day in the app on the days you use it, plus about ten minutes of forms across the whole fortnight.

What happens to my data?

We store your name, Google Play email and the goal you type, and we use them only to run this beta and email you about it. We don't sell or share them. Ask us to delete your application at any time by replying to any email from us — we'll do it and confirm. Inside the app you can export or delete everything from Settings.

Will you tell me if I'm not selected?

Yes. We're taking about twenty people. If you're not in this round we'll say so plainly rather than leaving you wondering, and you're welcome on the launch list.

Does it actually make things happen?

No, and any app claiming otherwise is selling you something. What it does is put you inside the life you want for two minutes and give you one small thing to do about it. Visualisation helps you notice and act on chances. That's the honest mechanism.
