STEP 1 — Create the 7 products in Play Console          (~20 min)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Nothing else can happen until these exist. RevenueCat imports FROM here.

Play Console -> your app -> Monetise with Play -> Products -> Subscriptions
-> "Create subscription" for each of these six:

  Product ID                          Name              Period   Price
  com.manifestai.standard.weekly      Standard Weekly   Weekly   $2.49
  com.manifestai.standard.monthly     Standard Monthly  Monthly  $5.99
  com.manifestai.standard.yearly      Standard Yearly   Yearly   $45.99
  com.manifestai.voice.weekly         Voice Weekly      Weekly   $6.99
  com.manifestai.voice.monthly        Voice Monthly     Monthly  $19.99
  com.manifestai.voice.yearly         Voice Yearly      Yearly   $179.99

For each one: enter the Product ID, give it a name, then add a BASE PLAN
with the billing period and price above. Then press ACTIVATE. A product
left as a draft does not exist as far as the app is concerned.

Then: Monetise with Play -> Products -> In-app products -> Create
  com.manifestai.standard.lifetime    Lifetime    $79.99
(This one is NOT a subscription — it is a single payment.)

WARNING: Product IDs are permanent. You cannot rename or reuse one.
Copy and paste them, do not type them.

STEP 2 — Create a Google service account                (~10 min)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
This is the fiddliest part. It lives in Google CLOUD Console, not Play
Console, which is why it's confusing.

  a) Play Console -> Setup -> API access
  b) If it asks you to link a Google Cloud project, do that first.
  c) Click "Create new service account". It opens Google Cloud Console
     in a new tab.
  d) In Google Cloud: Create Service Account.
     Name: revenuecat
     Click Create and Continue, then Done. (No roles needed here.)
  e) Click the new service account -> KEYS tab -> Add Key ->
     Create new key -> JSON -> Create.
     A .json file downloads. KEEP IT. This is a credential — do not
     commit it, do not paste its contents into any chat, including to me.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 3 — Give that service account access to your app   (~5 min)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  a) Back in Play Console -> Setup -> API access -> refresh.
     Your new service account now appears.
  b) Click "Manage Play Console permissions" next to it.
  c) Grant it access to your app with at least:
        View app information
        View financial data
        Manage orders and subscriptions
  d) Invite / Apply.

Note: Google can take up to 24 hours to propagate these permissions.
Usually it's minutes. If RevenueCat rejects the JSON in step 4, this is
almost always why — wait and retry rather than redoing it.

     Identifier EXACTLY:  premium
