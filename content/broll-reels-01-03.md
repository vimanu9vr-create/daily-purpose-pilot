# Three faceless B-roll reels

Under 15 seconds each, built to loop. No face, no voice, no performance.

**Where the footage comes from:** Pexels Videos — free, no attribution, commercial
use, and the app already pulls stills from Pexels so you know the source works.
Search "morning light window", "rain window night", "pouring water glass",
"scrolling phone dark". Download the vertical ones.

**Text rules, same as the daily reels:** body 60–74px, emphasis 100–130px, never
below 40px, text between y≈690 and y≈1450, one or two short lines per beat.

Post order: 3, then 1, then 2.

---

## 1 · The 6am one

*Habit tracking.*

**Visual loop** — tight on water being poured into a glass on a windowsill,
first light, slightly overexposed. Cut to a phone face-down on a bedside table.
Cut to bare feet on floorboards. Back to the pour. The pour is the loop point;
it reads as continuous.

**Audio** — ambient lo-fi with soft vinyl crackle. No drop, no vocal, nothing
that builds. This one shouldn't feel like it's going anywhere.

**On screen**

`You don't have a discipline problem.` → `You have a 6am problem.` → `The first
thing you touch decides the day.` → `Right now it's a screen.` → `Move the phone
tonight. That's it.` → `That's the whole habit.`

**Caption**

> The morning routine advice everyone gives you skips the only part that matters:
> what you touch first. Your 5am alarm doesn't fail because you're lazy — it
> fails because the phone is within arm's reach. Manifestation habit tracking
> starts with one decision made the night before. Link in bio.

## 2 · The camera roll one

*Vision boards.*

**Visual loop** — slow scroll through a photo grid, screen-lit in a dim room.
Decelerates and stops on one image: an apartment window, morning light, a plant.
A thumb hovers. The scroll resumes. Loops cleanly because a scroll has no
natural end.

**Audio** — cinematic piano, minor key, sparse. One sustained note under the
stop.

**On screen**

`Your camera roll already knows what you want.` → `The flat. The city. The desk
by the window.` → `You've opened it forty times.` → `You've shown nobody.` →
`That's not a vision board.` → `That's a secret.`

**Caption**

> Everyone has a screenshot they've looked at forty times and never mentioned to
> anyone. A vision board isn't decoration — it's the difference between a saved
> photo and a stated intention. Digital vision board, guided journaling and daily
> affirmations in one place. Link in bio.

## 3 · The four minutes one — post this first

*Audio affirmations + journaling.*

**Visual loop** — rain on a dark window. A warm lamp. A notebook open, pen
resting in the gutter. Coiled headphones. Nothing moves except the rain. This is
the strongest loop of the three: rain loops invisibly, so a replay is seamless
without any editing trick.

**Audio** — cinematic strings, very slow, nearly ambient. Rain noise if the
trending sound allows it.

**On screen**

`The last four minutes before you sleep decide tomorrow.` → `Not the eight
hours.` → `The four.` → `Whatever you're holding then, you wake up holding.` →
`Most people are holding a feed.` → `Hold one sentence instead.`

**Caption**

> The last few minutes before sleep are when your brain decides what tomorrow is
> about, and most of us hand that window to a feed. Night affirmations and guided
> journaling work because they occupy the slot, not because they're magic. Audio
> affirmations, journal prompts and habit tracking, free to start. Link in bio.

**Why this one first:** the loop is genuinely invisible, the claim is specific
and checkable rather than mystical, and *"most people are holding a feed"* is the
line somebody sends to a friend. Sends are what reach a cold audience.

## One note on the brief

Don't explicitly chase **Lucky Girl Syndrome**. The trend is from late 2022 and
most of what's now written about it is critical — it's become shorthand for toxic
positivity, and the backlash is documented. The aesthetic is exactly right; the
label will date you and drop you into the most crowded corner of the niche.

Take the look. Leave the name.
