# The daily hour

One hour. Same three things, every day. No decisions to make.

Both the workbook and the app are promoted by this hour, because they share one
funnel:

**video → profile → bio link → workbook page → thank-you page → app**

Bio link is `trymanifestationai.com/reset/` and nothing else.
A buyer meets the app automatically on the thank-you page. Someone who won't pay
takes the free Day One sample and leaves an email. You never choose which to
push.

---

## 1 · Post the video — 10 minutes

Upload the day's reel, set the cover, add a trending audio in the app, paste the
caption, post the first comment yourself.

Reply to every comment for the next three hours, even one-word ones.

## 2 · Twenty comments — 30 minutes

Go to the big manifestation accounts. Find twenty people who have written what
they actually want, in the comments, in their own words.

Write each one the specific affirmation you'd give them. One line. Free.

No link. No pitch. No "check my bio". The comment is the advert — the person
you just helped will click your name, and your bio does the rest.

This is the part that works when the algorithm won't. You don't need permission
to reach people who are already there.

## 3 · Five DMs — 20 minutes

Five creators between 5,000 and 50,000 followers who post consistently and have
nothing of their own to sell.

Put their stated goal into the app first. Send them what it wrote. Offer a free
lifetime account. Don't ask for a post.

Full scripts in `affiliate-outreach.md`.

---

## Once a week, not daily

**Sunday, ten minutes.** Open the insights on the week's posts and read two
numbers only: average watch time, and sends. Not views. Keep the shape of
whatever held attention longest; throw the rest away.

**Email whoever downloaded the free sample.** One idea, no hard sell.

---

## What not to do

Don't rebuild the page. Don't add a platform. Don't make a new product. Don't
switch payment processors. Don't redesign anything.

All of those feel like progress and none of them put you in front of a stranger.
When the hour feels pointless — and in week one it will — the temptation is to go
and improve something instead. That's the failure mode, and it's the only one
that actually guarantees nothing happens.

---

## What to expect

Weeks one and two: almost nothing. That's the normal shape, not a signal.

By week three the comments start producing profile visits, because you'll have
been useful to a few hundred people by then.

The number to watch is not followers. It's how many people land on the workbook
page. Ten sales of the $29 and we build the bundle.
