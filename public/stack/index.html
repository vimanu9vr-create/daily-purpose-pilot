Why it keeps not working

You are pouring clean water into a dusty glass.

The affirmation isn't the problem. The affirmation is fine. It is landing on a foundation that was never cleared — and that is why it tastes wrong every single time you say it.

"I am wealthy," said on a Tuesday by someone watching their account, is not a belief. It is a contradiction — and the mind resolves contradictions by quietly discarding the newer one.

  • You script in the morning and feel faintly dishonest by lunchtime.
  • You have repeated the same line for eleven days and felt nothing shift underneath it.
  • The vision board is still there. Nothing followed it.
  • You have started over four times, and each restart is quieter than the last.

The part nobody sells you

Every month you practise on an unaligned foundation, you are rehearsing the resistance instead.

Repetition doesn't care what it reinforces. Say a line your nervous system doesn't accept and you are not installing a new belief — you are practising the flinch that follows it. That flinch is the thing getting stronger.

This is why the fifth restart feels heavier than the first, and why more discipline has never once been the answer. The order is wrong. Clear the glass, then pour.
