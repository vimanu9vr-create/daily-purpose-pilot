You’ll finish the seven days. Then what?

Day 8 looks identical to Day 30 — a blank page asking you to start from nothing, on a Tuesday, when you’re tired. That is where almost everybody stops, and it has nothing to do with wanting it less.

So take the sentence you’re about to write on Day 1 and put it into the app. It does the part the paper can’t:

  • A new story every time you open it — the Day 2 visualisation, written fresh from your sentence, never picked off a shelf.
  • Affirmations in your words, not someone else’s, with one marked as the line to carry around all day.
  • Your letter, your obstacle and your streak, kept — so Day 30 starts from something instead of nothing.
  • Sleep sessions and meditations for the evenings you can’t face writing.
