# ManifestAI — what's left

Last updated 8 August 2026, after the migration off Lovable.

## What works, verified

Everything below has been seen working, not assumed.

Sign in with Google, on your own Supabase project. Onboarding saves and sticks.
The home feed, desires and stories. Sarah's voice, generated by ElevenLabs and
cached. Sessions that run their full advertised length over a real audio bed.
Frequency tones that are actually the frequency they claim. Push notifications
delivered to a phone. Fourteen tables with row-level security. Seven edge
functions deployed from the CLI, where a failure is visible instead of silent.

Nothing in the repo depends on Lovable. No subscription is required to run any
of it.

## Yours, and small

**Turn notifications on** in the app on this account. The old subscription
lived in the Lovable database and didn't come with you. Then tomorrow at 07:00
is a real unprompted test.

**Listen to a session end to end.** Still the one thing nobody has done. It
decides whether the writing is good enough to build a business on, and no
amount of code answers it.

## Mine, ready when you say

**Pre-generate the ten catalogue tracks.** The code is written and deployed —
catalogue audio is keyed by title rather than by user, so the first person to
play a track pays for it and everyone after gets it instantly. Running it once
costs about 24,000 characters of your ElevenLabs quota, forever, shared by
every future user. Worth doing before anyone else sees the app.

**Turn on Sentry.** The reporting is built and dormant. It needs a free DSN
from sentry.io in `.env` as `VITE_SENTRY_DSN`. Until then, errors only reach
the browser console, which means only you see them.

## Costs money, and gates the stores

Google Play is $25 once. The wait is the reason to start it early: a personal
developer account needs twelve testers opted in for fourteen continuous days
before you can publish. That clock costs nothing to start and is the longest
single item in the whole project.

Apple is $99 a year. Identity verification takes days. Needed for iOS at all,
and for Sign in with Apple, which App Store review requires once you offer
Google sign-in.

## Deliberately parked

Offline playback. Home screen widgets. A native rewrite in Flutter. A custom
domain, which matters before strangers see `pkxkksamenqcvsaulceq.supabase.co`
on Google's consent screen. RevenueCat products. None are urgent, all remain
available.

## The honest state

The app works. That sentence was not true a week ago, and it wasn't true this
morning either.

What's unproven is whether it's *good* — whether someone opens it tomorrow
because they want to rather than because they built it. Twenty-four thousand
words of guided audio exist and no human has listened to a session all the way
through.

That's the next milestone, and it isn't an engineering one.
