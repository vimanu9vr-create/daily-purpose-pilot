## Research, and what I could not verify

**Verified.** Watch time — total seconds including replays, not 3-second views —
remains Instagram's top-ranked signal into 2026, which is why the format stays
at 20 seconds and loops rather than chasing length. Sends carry the heaviest
weight for reaching people who don't follow you; likes matter mainly for the
audience you already have. Distribution starts with a small test pool and only
expands if that pool holds. Sources below.

**Not verified, and I won't pretend otherwise.** I can't log into Instagram or
TikTok, so I have not seen this account's view counts, watch-time curves or
which of Days 01–10 performed. Every judgement here about what to do next is
reasoned from the format and the research, not from your numbers. The moment
you paste real retention data, most of this becomes checkable and I'd rather
work from that.

I also can't verify which audio is currently trending — that library is

## Why this hook, after Day 09

Day 09 opened with **"Manifesting doesn't work."** This opens with the exact
reversal. That's deliberate, and it fixes a real weakness in the earlier one.

"Doesn't work" gives a sceptic permission to agree and scroll. They already
believed it, so nothing is learned and nothing is kept — the video wins the
first second and loses the next eighteen. **"Works, but not the way it's sold"
leaves a question standing that can only be closed by watching.** Agreement
ends a video; an unanswered question sustains one, and watch time is the signal
that matters.

It's also the rarer move. Conceding is unusual in a category built on promises,
and a concession gets read where an attack gets argued with.

**"Nothing is being sent to you" names the false mechanism exactly.** Not a
vague swipe at "the universe" — the specific belief being sold, which is
delivery. You can't replace a mechanism you haven't named.

**The recognition beat is a near-miss, not an accusation.** "You have already
walked past the thing you asked for" can't be checked either way, which makes
it much harder to dismiss than a confession would be. Day 09 told people when
they quit; this tells them what they missed, and nobody has said that to them
before.

**The turn concedes its own size.** "That's the whole trick" deliberately
undersells — a brand that has spent ten days refusing to overclaim can't
suddenly announce a secret. Small and true outperforms big and doubted.

**The saved line is the honest mechanism in six words.** *You don't attract it.
You start seeing it.* It's the only claim this product is allowed to make, it's
the sharpest counter-position available in this category, and it's short enough
to become a screenshot. Holds 4.6s, the longest beat, enforced at build time.

## Why this one should work

It's a concession, which is the least expected thing a manifestation account
can post, and concessions get read where attacks get argued with.

It hands over a screenshot rather than an opinion — and sends, not likes, are
what reach strangers. "You don't attract it. You start seeing it." is sendable
to one specific person, which is the only shape of content that travels on an
account with no audience.

And it never overclaims. The mechanism stated is attention and rehearsal,
nothing more. In a feed full of guaranteed outcomes, the honest version is now
the differentiated one.

**One caveat, plainly.** At under 10 followers, a very good video and a bad one
will both show you single-digit views for a while — Instagram seeds to your
followers first and you don't have any yet. Judge this on **sends per view and
average watch time as a percentage of length**, not on views. And post it to
TikTok too, where a cold account gets tested on strangers from the first upload.
