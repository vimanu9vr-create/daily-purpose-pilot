## Caption

"I am abundant" does nothing while your account says otherwise. That's not you
failing at manifesting — it's your own mind refusing to pretend, which is a
feature, not a fault.

An affirmation only survives contact with your own scepticism if it's about the
actual thing, in your actual words, specific enough to be slightly embarrassing.

That's the whole difference.
