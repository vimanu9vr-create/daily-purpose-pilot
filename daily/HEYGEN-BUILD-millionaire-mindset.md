## One thing to decide before posting

Two lines here conflict with the rules in `daily/FORMAT.md`:

- *"and watch your money change"* is an outcome promise, which that file rules
  out — and which is also a rejection risk if this is ever boosted.
- *"Cash flows into my life effortlessly"* and *"Money is unlimited, just like
  the air I breathe"* are the generic affirmations Day 02 of the workbook
  explicitly tells people to stop saying.

ManifestAI's whole differentiator is being the honest one in this category.
This reel sells the opposite, so anyone who clicks through meets a different
product from the one the reel promised.

Minimal fix if you want it: *"and watch what you start noticing"* — six words,
same rhythm, no promise. Shipping it as-is is a legitimate choice too, but it
should be a choice.
