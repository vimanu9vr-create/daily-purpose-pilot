# HeyGen script — "The fastest way to manifest money"

Format taken from the three Stella reels: talking head to camera, one-phrase
subtitles, affirmations spoken aloud, a cut to the app screen, then "my app."
The structure is theirs. The script is not — theirs assert affirmations, this
one earns them by naming why the generic ones failed first. That argument is
the only thing you have that they don't.

---

## HeyGen setup

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | Avatar Video |
| Aspect | 9:16 portrait |
| Avatar | Female, warm indoor setting, seated. Avoid office/boardroom avatars |
| Voice | Calm unhurried female |
| Speed | **0.95** — sounds like thinking, not reading |
| Captions | ON. Large, 1–2 words at a time, white with dark outline, centre-low |
| Length | ~26 seconds |

The subtitle rhythm is doing half the retention work in the Stella videos.
Don't skip it, and don't let HeyGen put full sentences on screen.

## THE SCRIPT — paste exactly this into HeyGen

```
What's the fastest way to manifest money?

Not by saying "I am a millionaire."

You've said that one in the car. Nothing moved.

And then you felt slightly stupid, and you stopped by Thursday.

The problem was never your belief. It was the sentence.

Say something small enough to picture.

I pay for it without checking the balance.

The number goes up while I'm asleep.

I walk into rooms without checking if I belong.

Then say it again tomorrow. That's the part everybody skips.

I use my app, ManifestAI. You type the goal, and it writes the line.
```

## The one edit that matters

Download the HeyGen render, open CapCut, and **overlay your app recording from
24.5s to the end** — the moment she says "I use my app." Use
`ManifestAI_Day14_Demo.mp4`, which already shows the goal being typed and the
affirmation appearing. Keep the HeyGen audio running underneath.

This is exactly what Stella does at "using my app Stella." The face builds
trust; the screen proves the product exists. Neither works alone.

If you want it stronger, also cut to the app for 2 seconds at **15.5s**, on
"I pay for it without checking the balance" — showing that line on an
affirmation card while she says it. Said and shown at once is the most
convincing two seconds available to you.

## Two honest notes

**HeyGen's free tier watermarks everything.** Render one free first to judge
whether the avatar reads as credible here. Manifestation audiences are
unusually sensitive to authenticity, and a synthetic presenter fronting a
personal-practice brand is the real risk in this format — not the cost.

**Filming yourself does the same job for nothing**, with the one thing no
avatar has: you built it. Same script, same subtitle style, phone on a stack
of books by a window. If the HeyGen version works, that's strong evidence the
self-filmed one would work better.
