## What I changed and why

The brief had you say, in the first person, that you manifested millions by
repeating *"money comes to me abundantly and freely."*

**That's a fabricated earnings testimonial.** Beyond the legal exposure — claims
about money outcomes are the single most regulated thing in this category — it
breaks two things you can't afford to break.

**It contradicts page one of what it sells.** Your sales page says: *"'I am a
millionaire' does nothing while your account says otherwise. You aren't
persuading yourself. You are arguing with yourself, and you always lose."* The
video would argue the opposite. Someone watches it, clicks, and reads you
disagreeing with yourself at the exact moment they're deciding whether to trust
you with $29.

**And it's the same line Day 02 told people to stop saying.** "Money comes to me
abundantly and freely" is a textbook generic affirmation — the thing that video
was entirely about. Six videos later you'd be selling it back. A stranger who
sees both learns your advice isn't stable, and on an account this size that's
the most expensive thing they could learn.

There's an audience problem too. That script pulls in people who want the
universe to handle it. They don't buy an honest workbook. They buy it, find
page one telling them wishing doesn't work, and leave the review that ends the
product.

**The format was right and I kept all of it.** Question hook, direct personal
answer, one line people screenshot. Only the answer is different — and it's
still an affirmation they can save, just one that survives contact with their
own disbelief. That's Day 6 of the workbook: *one line you can say to a mirror
without cringing, the single constraint that makes affirmations work at all.*

**"You stopped by Thursday" is the beat that does the work.** It's the most
specific line in the video and the one most likely to make someone feel caught
rather than lectured. Everything before it earns that; everything after it
offers a way out.

**The saved line holds for 4.4 seconds — the longest beat, by design.** It's the
only frame anyone screenshots, and a screenshot needs time to become one.
There's a build-time assert that no other beat is ever allowed to run longer,
so a later edit can't quietly demote it.

**Why "I have made money before. I can make it again."** It's true for almost
everyone watching — a wage, a sale, a refund, a first job. That's the whole
trick: your mind can't reject it, so it doesn't start the argument. It's also
unfalsifiable in the honest direction, which the abundance line is not.

## Caption

Can you manifest millions? Not by saying "money flows to me freely."

You've tried that one. You said it in the mirror, your bank balance disagreed
in real time, and you quietly stopped by Thursday. That isn't a discipline
problem. Your mind refused to pretend, which is the correct response to being
told something it can see isn't true.

An affirmation only does anything if you can't argue with it. So the line isn't
"I am wealthy." It's this:

*I have made money before. I can make it again.*

You can't reject that one. You have evidence. And a sentence you can't argue
with is a sentence you'll still be saying on day six — which is the only thing
that has ever mattered.
