## Research, and what I could not verify

**Verified, this week:**

- Watch time is the top signal and counts replays — a 15s reel watched three times outranks a 60s watched once. Sends per reach carry roughly 3–5× the weight of likes for reaching non-followers, which is the only lever that matters on an account with 11 followers.
- Reels above ~70% average watch time get pushed to Explore; below 50% distribution drops sharply.
- The stay-or-scroll decision lands at about **1.7 seconds** on Instagram, which is harder on slow intros than TikTok is.
- Pattern interrupt is still the strongest hook family. Scroll-past rates rose from 62% to 78% between 2024 and 2025 — audiences now recognise and dismiss formula hooks faster, so a hook that looks like manifestation content is dead on arrival.
- The backlash against manifestation as toxic positivity is active and documented: the specific criticism is that repeating affirmations that don't feel true suppresses real feeling.

**Could not verify:** I cannot log into Instagram and cannot see view counts on any individual Reel. Nothing here claims one. I also cannot confirm which audio is trending — the library is mobile-only — so the audio direction below describes a mood rather than naming a track.

**Winner: "Stop saying 'I am abundant.'"** Four words plus a quote, inside the 1.7-second window. A manifestation account telling you to *stop affirming* doesn't match the format of the feed it appears in, and that mismatch has to be resolved before anyone can comfortably scroll. It also names the exact sentence a large part of this audience has actually said out loud, which is why it beats "your affirmations aren't working" — that one is a category, this one is a quote from their own bathroom mirror.

Light arc: cold window high-left while the problem is stated, warm lamp growing from beat 4. The light does the emotional work the words aren't allowed to overclaim.

## Why this one should work

The saturated lane is writing more affirmations. This attacks a specific sentence most of the audience has actually said, names the real reason it failed, and hands back the replacement in the app's own words. The recognition beat gives them something to argue with in the comments, and the comment prompt turns that argument into a demo.

The honest caveat: beat 1 is a command, and commands get scrolled by people who don't like being told what to do. If watch time underperforms two days running, the format note says test the same script shot on a phone or handwritten on paper before changing anything else — raw is currently outperforming polished, and this account's look is deliberately premium.
